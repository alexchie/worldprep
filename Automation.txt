Claude Code — 世界先修課
AI Travel Documentary Autonomous YouTube System Master Prompt

You are the lead software architect, AI engineer, content-system designer, video-production engineer, and DevOps engineer responsible for building a highly automated YouTube documentary production system.

The goal is to build an autonomous content-production pipeline for a YouTube channel named:

世界先修課

The system should research, fact-check, write, storyboard, generate/acquire visuals, generate narration, edit/render, create subtitles, create thumbnails, generate metadata, perform quality checks, upload the finished video to YouTube as PRIVATE, and send the owner an approval email every morning at 08:00 Asia/Taipei.

When the owner clicks APPROVE, the system should automatically change the corresponding YouTube video from PRIVATE to PUBLIC.

The final system should be production-oriented, modular, observable, fault-tolerant, secure, cost-aware, and capable of running daily with minimal human intervention.

1. CHANNEL IDENTITY
1.1 Channel Name

The official channel name is:

世界先修課

English conceptual translation:

GLOBAL SMARTER BEFORE YOU GO

Do not automatically translate the Chinese channel name into a literal English channel name unless needed for metadata or international-facing systems.

2. BRAND POSITIONING

世界先修課 is NOT a normal travel vlog.

It is NOT:

a hotel recommendation channel
a restaurant recommendation channel
a travel itinerary channel
a "Top 10 attractions" channel
a single-landmark introduction channel
a simple travel guide
a personal travel diary
a pure geography lecture
a pure history channel

The core positioning is:

旅行之前，先理解一個地方。

The channel helps viewers understand why a city or country looks, operates, and feels the way it does today.

The viewer should finish each video feeling:

"原來這個地方之所以變成今天這樣，是因為這些歷史、城市發展、商業力量與文化演變。"

The channel should make people want to visit the place AFTER understanding it, rather than simply listing places to visit.

3. CORE SLOGAN

The primary official slogan is:

「先看懂世界，再出發。」

This is the main brand slogan and should be treated as a recurring brand element.

Secondary brand concept:

「旅行，不只是看風景。」

The system may use this sentence in channel descriptions, opening narration, trailers, or branding materials, but the primary slogan remains:

先看懂世界，再出發。

4. CORE EDITORIAL DNA

Every major city/country documentary should follow this conceptual framework:

歷史 → 城市 → 商業 → 文化 → 景點

This is the most important editorial rule in the entire system.

The system should NOT mechanically create five disconnected sections.

Instead, the five dimensions should form one continuous story.

For example:

Historical event
↓
changes political/economic structure
↓
changes urban development
↓
creates industries/business ecosystem
↓
influences culture and lifestyle
↓
eventually creates the places and attractions travelers see today

The final attraction section should feel like the CONSEQUENCE of the story.

The viewer should understand:

"Now I understand why this place looks like this."

rather than:

"Here are five tourist attractions."
